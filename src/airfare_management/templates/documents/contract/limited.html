{# 9 Termination #}

9) Termination of the contract:

Each of the parties of the contract may terminate the contract during the probationary period after giving a one-day notice to the other party, and at least a 30-days’ notice after the end of the probationary period. The Company may terminate this contract immediately without notification or indemnity or end of service benefits based on the cases stated in Article 107 of the Labour Law No. 36/2012.

٩) فسخ العقد

{# 14 Obligations #}

14) Employee obligations:

The employee must comply with the provisions of the implemented Labour Law, in particular:

a- He must be present in the work place at the scheduled times, and that the work entrusted to him must be performed with the same care as a normal person.

b- To abide by the conditions and instructions of occupational safety, and the means of prevention that must be followed in the company.

c- To respect the rules and regulations of the company in which he works.

١٤) التزامات الموظف

يجب على الموظف الإلتزام بأحكام قانون العمل المنفذة وعلى الأخص:

أ) أن يتواجد في مكان العمل في المواعيد المقررة وأن العمل المنوط له يبذل في تأديته من العناية ما يبذله الشخص المعتاد.

ب) أن يتقيد بشروط وتعليمات السلامة المهنية، ووسائل الوقاية الواجب إتباعها بالشركة.

ج) أن يحترم الأنظمة واللوائح المرعية بالشركة التي يعمل بها.
